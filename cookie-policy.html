1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide a better browsing experience, and give website operators useful information.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (which expire when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them). This Cookie Policy explains how Beats Audio ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in, or filling in forms. Without these cookies, our site would not work properly.

Always Active

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, such as which pages are visited most often and if visitors encounter error messages. This data helps us improve website performance and user experience. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and anonymous.

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences, language selections, and cart contents. They may be set by us or by third-party services we have added to our pages (e.g., live chat, embedded media). If you do not allow these cookies, some or all of these services may not function properly.

Targeting & Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads on other sites. They do not directly store personal information but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and device.

Each third party has its own privacy and cookie policy. We encourage you to review their policies for more information.

5. Managing Your Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in the following ways:

5.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically find these settings in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu of your browser. The following links may help:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

5.2 Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For advertising cookies, you can visit the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page or the Network Advertising Initiative's opt-out page.

6. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we use localStorage and sessionStorage (HTML5 Web Storage) to store information on your device. These technologies serve similar purposes to cookies but can store larger amounts of data and are not automatically sent to the server with each request. For example, we use localStorage to persist your shopping cart contents and sessionStorage for the promotional countdown timer. You can clear this data through your browser's developer tools or settings.

7. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable or block cookies, some features of our website may not function properly. For example, you may not be able to add items to your cart, complete a purchase, or have your preferences remembered. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled as they are essential for the basic operation of the site.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
